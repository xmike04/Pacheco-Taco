# LogoUsage

Which mark goes where, shown on its approved grounds.

- **Primary lockup** (`pacheco-lockup.svg`) — site header, menus, receipts. Cream or tortilla ground only.
- **Reverse lockup** — on chile red. **Dark lockup** — on ink/char.
- **Seal** — footer, stickers, cups, merch, social avatar ring.
- **P monogram** — app icon, Instagram/Facebook avatar, favicon (`pacheco-favicon.svg` at 32/48px).
- **One-colour ink** — single-colour print, stamps, embroidery, laser-etched boards.
- Clear space = the height of the "N" disc on every side. Minimum width: lockup 140px, seal 72px.
- Don't: recolour outside the palette, stretch, add soft shadows, or set "Pacheco" in a different script.
