# Hero

The first screen: chile ground, papel picado garland along the top, one headline, one line of subcopy, two actions.

**Consumer provides:** a kicker (location), an ALL-CAPS headline of 3–6 words with at most one `pt-script` word, one sentence of subcopy, and two buttons (`pt-btn--queso` for ordering, `pt-btn--ghost` for directions). Optionally a cut-out food photo on the right (PNG, transparent background, with `shadow-sticker`).

- The papel picado image (`Patterns/papel-picado.svg`) tiles horizontally; keep it 60–80px tall.
- Never put body text over a photo; the chile ground carries all text.
