# InfoCard

Location, hours and phone in one strip — the three things a hungry visitor scrolls for.

**Consumer provides:** address (linked to Google Maps), hours as displayed on Google Business Profile, and a `tel:` link. Keep these identical to Google/Yelp (NAP consistency matters for local search).

- Icons are `pt-icon` masks in `chile`, so they follow the theme.
- Stacks to one column under 720px.
- The phone number in the preview is a placeholder — replace before launch.
